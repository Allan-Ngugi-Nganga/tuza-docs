# Tuza docs

Tuza puts a viewer's tip on your stream while they are still watching. They pay with
M-Pesa, the alert appears, and the money is yours minus the fee you agreed to.

This site is the manual. It assumes you can open OBS and click a button, and nothing
more.

## If you get stuck

The two questions that answer most problems are which browser you are on and which
version of OBS you are running. Have those ready, then write to
[allan@ngugi.work](mailto:allan@ngugi.work) with a screenshot if you can.
