# Tuza docs

Tuza puts a viewer's tip on your stream while they are still watching. They pay with
M-Pesa, the alert appears, and the money is yours minus the fee you agreed to.

This site is the manual. It assumes you can open OBS and click a button, and nothing
more.

## Start with these three

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **[Your overlay](overlay.md)**

    One link, pasted into OBS once. After that, every tip appears on your stream by
    itself.

- **[Sounds and alerts](sounds.md)**

    Pick what a tip sounds like, upload your own, and decide which gifts are worth
    an alert.

- **[Money](money.md)**

    What a viewer pays, what you keep, and when it reaches your phone.

</div>
