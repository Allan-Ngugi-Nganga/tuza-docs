# Money

Here is the whole path, so you can decide before you start.

## What a viewer pays

A viewer opens your tip page, enters an amount and their M-Pesa number, and confirms the
prompt on their phone. The money goes to Tuza's payment processor, Paystack, which is
licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya to collect it. Tuza never holds the card or the
M-Pesa PIN.

## What you keep

Every tip shows its own arithmetic in your activity feed: the amount sent, the M-Pesa
fee, the Tuza fee, and what landed. Nothing is deducted invisibly at the end of the
month.

On the free plan you keep 93.50 of every 100. Pro takes less, and the fee is on your
dashboard rather than in an email.

## When it arrives

Two clocks, both real:

- **Settlement.** Paystack releases collected money on T+2, two working days after the
  tip. That timing is the processor's, and no amount of software shortens it.
- **Payout.** Once the money is available, Tuza pays it to your M-Pesa number.

Payouts are run manually while Tuza's business verification with Paystack is being
completed. That means a payout can take a day or two longer than it will later. It is a
temporary limit, and it is stated here rather than discovered by you.

The minimum payout is **KES 500**, because a KES 100 transfer loses a fifth of itself to
the wallet fee. Below that, Tuza batches instead of paying per tip.

## What you will never see

No subscription taken from your tips without you choosing it. No fee that appears after
the fact. And no balance that shows money you cannot move yet: money inside the
settlement window is shown separately from money you can withdraw.
